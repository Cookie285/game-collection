# Collecting rules

**Physical first.** Digital only when there's no other option.

## Platform choice

Priority across platforms: **PC** (best graphics) → **Nintendo** (best physical line-up) → whatever is still exclusive on **PlayStation** or **Xbox**.

| Platform | Role |
|---|---|
| **Switch** | First choice for JRPGs and for anything that has a real Switch cart. |
| **Switch 2 Game-Key Card** | Does **not** count as physical for non-JRPGs → take the PlayStation disc (precedent: Pragmata, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, Full Metal Schoolgirl on PS5). JRPGs whose only Switch version is a Game-Key Card → case by case (precedent: Bravely Default HD Remaster, Brigandine Abyss, Granblue Fantasy: Relink bought as GKC). |
| **PC** | Steam sales only, no physical PC games. |
| **PlayStation** | Exclusives + multiplatform games worth having on disc. |
| **Xbox** | Shooter console (full Call of Duty + Battlefield runs) and Microsoft first-party on disc (Disc-to-Digital licence), even for games also released elsewhere. The shooter rule beats Nintendo-first for shooter series (Switch shooter ports are usually the weakest version). |

- Microsoft games whose only disc is on PS5 are bought on PS5 (Gears of War: Reloaded only when very cheap; Halo: Campaign Evolved bought on PS5; Fable pre-ordered on PS5). Otherwise: don't buy another platform's disc just to fill an Xbox gap.
- Tie-break idea (suggested, **not adopted**): if a series has at least one Xbox-exclusive entry, keep the whole run on Xbox.
- Xbox details: see [Xbox](#xbox) below.

## Copies, formats, condition

- **One copy per game.** For cross-gen releases take the **newest-gen** version (e.g. Battlefield Hardline on Xbox One, not 360).
- **Compilations / remasters on a newer gen replace the originals** (e.g. God of War Collection instead of the PS2 discs); older originals only where no newer disc exists.
- **Already owned on Steam** → buy the disc only if the price is really good, new or used. No double-buying otherwise.
- Gaps are mostly bought **used**.
- **Single-player focus** — multiplayer-only / online-focused games are skipped (Destiny, Overwatch, Crash Team Rumble); dead servers don't matter. No peripheral games (Guitar Hero, Skylanders).
- **Compilations count** as owning their games in series runs (MW Trilogy → CoD 4 / MW2 / MW3; MCC → Halo CE–4; Gears UE → Gears of War; Year One → State of Decay; God of War Collection → GoW I + II).
- Limited Run / boutique pressings count as real discs, but only at a good price.
- No price limits set yet.

## Xbox

### Physical vs digital
- **Real disc** (incl. disc + day-one patch / partial install) → buy; this is the collection.
- **Code-in-a-box** → only sealed/new, only at or below the current Xbox Store sale price, EU/DACH retail box; redeem immediately; tag as code-in-box in CLZ; doesn't count toward disc runs (no disc, no Disc-to-Digital, no resale).
- **Digital-only** → play on Game Pass first; buy only if keeping/replaying or if a third-party title leaves Game Pass; prefer Xbox Store with Play Anywhere, else Steam sale.
- **No Xbox retail disc exists** → out of Xbox disc scope ("layer X"), regardless of platform.

### Xbox 360 / OG Xbox backward compatibility
- Buy most BC titles **digitally in Xbox Store sales** (no key sellers for 360 titles).
- On disc instead: titles **delisted** from the store (disc is the only way), and **Gears of War 2 / 3 / Judgment** for Gears shelf consistency.
- Check Full Library first (some 360 games are free or were Games with Gold, e.g. Crackdown 1/2, Too Human).
- Prefer GOTY/Ultimate disc editions where DLC is on the disc (Fallout 3, New Vegas).
- Non-BC discs (PGR, Forza Motorsport 1–4, Jet Set Radio Future, DOA, Amped, Steel Battalion, Kinect titles) are shelf-only → not collecting.

### First-party scope
- Layer 1 = published by Microsoft / Xbox Game Studios at release · layer 2 = Bethesda/ZeniMax titles published after the March 2021 acquisition · layer 3 = studio owned by Microsoft today but game published by someone else at release (optional). Default: layers 1–2.
- Sep 2026 Xbox "reset" doesn't change what counts (historical publisher counts); future Halo, Rare, World's Edge games ship under Activision; Undead Labs, Double Fine, Compulsion are independent now.
- Activision/Blizzard pre-Oct-2023 titles = layer 3; normal platform rules per game (Switch first for Crash/Spyro/Tony Hawk/Diablo II/III).

### Disc-to-Digital
- Only Xbox One / Series X discs are eligible (not 360 / OG Xbox). One revocable licence per disc; the licence moves if the disc is claimed on another account → don't lend discs casually.
- Insider access; tested: the Call of Duty: Infinite Warfare disc unlocked both IW and the bundled Modern Warfare Remastered → bundle sub-licences convert too.
- Community (Sep 2026): delisted Forza titles (FM5/6/7, FH3/4) not working (car licensing) — re-test after general availability; Deadpool, Titanfall, Rock Band 4, F1 2021, Minecraft: Story Mode reported working; Transformers: Rise of the Dark Spark and The Amazing Spider-Man 2 not working. Forza discs were bought hoping they'll work later.

### Not collecting (Xbox)
- Third-party "complete only on Xbox" series: Valve set (Orange Box, L4D 1–2, Portal 2), Dead Space, Max Payne, Lost Planet, Splinter Cell, Saints Row, Dead Rising (as a series), Condemned, Mercenaries, MechAssault. Exception: Titanfall 2 for completion.
- Rare Replay disc (owned digitally), Halo 3/ODST/Reach/4 360 discs (covered by MCC), Gears of War 360 (covered by UE), MW Remastered standalone disc (covered via IW bundle), MW2 Campaign Remastered (digital-only).

## Hardware constraints

- Consoles: backward-compatible **PS3** (plays PS1 + PS2 discs), **PS5 Pro** (plays PS4 + PS5 discs), **Xbox Series X** (plays OG Xbox / 360 BC titles + One + Series discs).
- PS1/PS2 discs are region-locked on the PS3 → **PAL only**. PS3/PS4/PS5 discs are practically region-free.
- **PSP / PS Vita** games can't be played → not collected; the ones owned are kept as collection pieces.

## Next PlayStation

- Most likely **not** buying the next PlayStation (no Disc-to-Digital; Sony's recent moves seen as anti-consumer) — unless a very big exclusive changes that (*Intergalactic* on watch).
- So: fill the PlayStation collection while discs are still produced; buy used copies for older gaps.

## Future-proofing

- Worry: PC and Nintendo may end up the only platforms with open/physical options if PS and Xbox go digital-only.
- Digital 360 BC licences likely outlive discs if the next Xbox has no drive; the Series X is the last machine that reads four generations of Xbox discs.

## Tracking & shops

- **CLZ Games** is where games are scanned/catalogued → CSV export → this repo.
- Steam library (~1,176 games) is **not** part of the collection rows; Steam ownership is noted per game in [`data/annotations.csv`](data/annotations.csv) and in target/series notes.
- Shops: rebuy.de, netgames (cheap new), eBay saved searches (Limited Run releases: Pentiment, Grounded).
- Xbox: Game Pass active, likes Xbox Play Anywhere; Xbox Insider access to Disc-to-Digital (IW Legacy disc also unlocks MW Remastered — tested).
